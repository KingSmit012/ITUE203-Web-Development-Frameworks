University Academic Circular

Subject: Mid-Term Examination Schedule - Academic Year 2026-27

All 3rd Semester Computer Engineering and IT students are hereby informed that the mid-semester internal examinations will commence from 1st August 2026.

  • Hall tickets will be generated on 25th July 2026.
  • Students must maintain minimum 75% attendance to appear in exams.
  • Seating arrangement will be posted in the Examination section.

For any queries, please contact your academic mentor or department office.
